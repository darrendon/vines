How to play

Placing. On your turn, place one card's piece (rotations and flips allowed). A piece is legal if it covers a bottom-row square (side A) or touches a seed square (side B), or if it edge-touches at least two other pieces and at least one of them is the same colour.

Running out. If none of your cards can be placed anywhere, you are out for the round and keep your cards. Empty your hand and you remove your largest penalty token.

Scoring. When nobody can play, each card left in hand costs 1 point plus 1 per uncovered flower of its colour (the five flowers in the soil can never be covered, which is where the 1 comes from). Any piece may cover any flower. Lowest total after all rounds wins.

Deck. Seven pieces per colour (F, L, N, P, V, Y, Z) plus one extra blue V, which is removed with five players. Two players deal 5 each and draw a card after every placement; the round ends as soon as one player can't play, and everyone scores the cards left in hand.

Online tables. The host's device runs the game; friends join by entering the five-letter code. Messages go through a public relay, so everyone just needs the page open. If you refresh, rejoin with the same code and name to get your seat back.
